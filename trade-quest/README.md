# Trade Quest

A Duolingo-style, mobile-first PWA that teaches news-driven **swing trading** — holding stocks for days to weeks around news and catalysts — from zero to a proficient, disciplined process. 42 days, practiced with paper (simulated) money first.

## The 42-day curriculum

- **Week 1 — Market Foundations (Days 1–7):** how markets work, order types for swing trades (and why gaps can jump past stops), accounts and rules (incl. the June 2026 end of the Pattern Day Trader rule), real costs and extended-hours sessions, the honest research on trading, why news-driven swing trading, paper-trading setup.
- **Week 2 — Reading a Stock & Its Fundamentals (Days 8–14):** quote pages, the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow, valuation, earnings reports and guidance, SEC filings on EDGAR, a one-page company scorecard.
- **Week 3 — Charts for Swing Traders (Days 15–21):** candlesticks and timeframes, trend, support and resistance, volume, moving averages, indicators, top-down analysis and watchlists.
- **Week 4 — News, Catalysts & Swing Setups (Days 22–28):** what moves stocks, reading news like a trader, the macro calendar and market context, pullback / breakout / post-earnings-gap setups, event risk, a news-driven trading plan and daily routine.
- **Week 5 — Risk, Psychology & Career (Days 29–35):** position sizing for gap risk, R-multiples and expectancy, journaling, psychology, taxes, leverage and options pitfalls, a career path and 90-day plan.
- **Week 6 — Quality Stocks: Own Them, Swing Them (Days 36–42):** a quality-company scorecard, moats, management and capital allocation, growth potential, a SpaceX (SPCX) and hot-IPO case study, owning a long-term core while swing trading around it (incl. lot selection, wash-sale and IRA traps), and an own-anyway watchlist.

Each day: intro → 5 lesson cards → a mission using free sources (EDGAR, company IR pages, free charts) or paper trading — never real money → 5-question quiz. XP, streaks, stars, a Practice queue for missed questions, and a Handbook of unlocked lessons. Progress saves on-device; installs as a PWA and works offline.

Rules, fees, and figures were last fact-checked in November 2026. Educational only — not financial or investment advice. Trading involves substantial risk of loss.
