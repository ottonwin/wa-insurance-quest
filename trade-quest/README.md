# Trade Quest

A Duolingo-style, mobile-first PWA that takes a beginner to a proficient, disciplined understanding of US stock swing trading and day trading — structured as a 30-day course, practiced with paper (simulated) money first.

## The 30-day curriculum

- **Week 1 — Market Foundations (Days 1–7):** how markets work, order types, accounts and rules (including the June 2026 end of the Pattern Day Trader rule), brokers and real costs, the honest research on trading careers, trading styles, paper-trading setup.
- **Week 2 — Reading Charts (Days 8–14):** candlesticks and timeframes, trend and market structure, support and resistance, volume, moving averages and VWAP, indicators, multi-timeframe analysis.
- **Week 3 — Setups & Strategies (Days 15–21):** swing pullbacks, breakouts, trading the open, VWAP and mean reversion, catalysts and scanners, short selling, writing a trading plan.
- **Week 4 — Risk, Psychology & Career (Days 22–30):** position sizing, R-multiples and expectancy, journaling and backtesting, psychology, taxes, leverage and options awareness, prop firms, building the career, a 90-day proficiency plan.

Each day: intro → 5 lesson cards → a paper-trading or chart-study mission (never real money) → 5-question quiz. XP, streaks, stars, a Practice queue for missed questions, and a Handbook of unlocked lessons. Progress saves on-device; installs as a PWA and works offline.

Rules, fees, and figures were last fact-checked in November 2026. Educational only — not financial or investment advice. Trading involves substantial risk of loss.
